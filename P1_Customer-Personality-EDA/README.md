## Dataset

**Source:**  
*Kaggle – Customer Personality Analysis*  
🔗 https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/imakash3011/customer-personality-analysis/data

This dataset contains detailed information on customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and responses to marketing campaigns.

### 1. People
- **ID**: Unique customer identifier  
- **Year_Birth**: Customer’s year of birth  
- **Education**: Education level  
- **Marital_Status**: Marital status  
- **Income**: Yearly household income  
- **Kidhome / Teenhome**: Number of children and teenagers in the household  
- **Dt_Customer**: Customer enrollment date  
- **Recency**: Days since last purchase  
- **Complain**: Complaint indicator (1 = Yes, 0 = No)

---

### 2. Product Spend (Last 2 Years)
- **MntWines, MntFruits, MntMeatProducts**
- **MntFishProducts, MntSweetProducts, MntGoldProds**

---

### 3. Promotion (Campaign Engagement)
- **NumDealsPurchases**: Discount-based purchases  
- **AcceptedCmp1 – AcceptedCmp5**: Campaign acceptance indicators  
- **Response**: Acceptance of most recent campaign

---

### 4. Place (Purchase Channels & Digital Activity)
- **NumWebPurchases**
- **NumCatalogPurchases**
- **NumStorePurchases**
- **NumWebVisitsMonth**
