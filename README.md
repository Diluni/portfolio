### Project 1: Customer Personality Analysis — EDA & Marketing Insights
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-150458?style=flat&logo=pandas&logoColor=white)
![Matplotlib](https://img.shields.io/badge/Matplotlib-11557C?style=flat&logo=matplotlib&logoColor=white)
![Seaborn](https://img.shields.io/badge/Seaborn-4C72B0?style=flat&logoColor=white)

#### Overview
Performed end-to-end exploratory data analysis on a 2,000+ customer dataset using a marketing framework (People, Product, Place, Promotions) to uncover actionable customer insights that help organizations move from broad outreach to targeted, data-driven campaign strategies.

#### Key Contributions
- Engineered demographic and behavioral features including family size, customer tenure, and recency-based engagement categories
- Analyzed spending patterns across six product categories, identifying wines and meat as the highest-spend segments
- Evaluated marketing campaign acceptance rates across the customer base
- Mapped purchase channel distribution across in-store, web, and catalog touchpoints

#### Outcomes
- Revealed broadly low campaign engagement (~16% acceptance rate), flagging the need for more targeted and personalized campaign strategies
- Identified a small but highly responsive customer segment (~1.6%) that accepted four or more campaigns, presenting an opportunity for loyalty-focused and high-engagement campaign strategies
- Found that the majority of customers have taken advantage of at least one discount, while very few utilized more than five deals, suggesting discount-driven promotions have baseline traction but limited depth
- Identified in-store as the dominant purchase channel (~46%) with a strong secondary signal in web (~33%) and high website visit frequency among engaged customers
- Delivered a clean, feature-rich dataset ready for downstream clustering and customer personalization modeling

## Project 2: Customer Segmentation Analysis — Clustering
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Pandas](https://img.shields.io/badge/Pandas-150458?style=flat&logo=pandas&logoColor=white)
![Scikit-Learn](https://img.shields.io/badge/Scikit--Learn-F7931E?style=flat&logo=scikit-learn&logoColor=white)
![Matplotlib](https://img.shields.io/badge/Matplotlib-11557C?style=flat&logo=matplotlib&logoColor=white)
![Seaborn](https://img.shields.io/badge/Seaborn-4C72B0?style=flat&logoColor=white)

### Overview
Conducted a comprehensive customer segmentation analysis using K-Means clustering to identify four distinct customer groups based on demographic attributes, purchasing behavior, and campaign engagement. Transforms raw customer data into actionable segments that equip organizations with the customer intelligence needed to design targeted, data-driven marketing strategies and support business decision-making.

### Key Contributions
- Applied K-Means clustering to segment customers into four behaviorally and demographically distinct profiles
- Profiled each cluster across key dimensions including income, family size, age, tenure, spending, purchase frequency, campaign responsiveness, and recency
- Translated cluster characteristics into business-relevant labels and strategic recommendations for each segment
- Developed an executive-level summary mapping each cluster to a clear strategic focus for marketing and retention planning

### Outcomes

#### Cluster Profiles & Business Insights

| Cluster | Name | Profile Summary | Strategic Focus |
|--------|------|----------------|-----------------|
| 0 | **Affluent Loyalists** | Highest spenders, most campaign-responsive, long-term loyal customers | Protect and maximize value |
| 1 | **Budget-Conscious Families** | Lowest income and spending, large families, least campaign-responsive | Engage through affordability |
| 2 | **Legacy Households** | Moderate spenders, older customers, family-oriented with growth potential | Nurture and grow |
| 3 | **Selective High Earners** | High income, frequent buyers, low campaign engagement, at risk of churn | Re-activate and retain |

#### Key Strategic Outcomes
- **Affluent Loyalists** identified as the highest revenue-driving segment — recommended for premium offers, loyalty rewards, and early-access campaigns to protect and maximize lifetime value
- **Budget-Conscious Families** found to be price-sensitive with low campaign responsiveness — value-based bundles, family-oriented promotions, and essentials-focused messaging recommended to drive engagement
- **Legacy Households** present a growth opportunity through moderate but consistent engagement — targeted seasonal campaigns and gradual upsell strategies recommended to deepen spend
- **Selective High Earners** flagged as a retention risk due to longer gaps between purchases despite high income — personalized re-engagement and win-back campaigns prioritized over broad mass promotions
- Segmentation output provides a directly actionable framework for moving from one-size-fits-all campaigns to personalized, segment-specific marketing strategies across the customer base
